## C. Numbering without leading zeros

- No zero-padded counters existed in the data (the old site has none in the extracted strings).
- The only zero-padded numbers were derived by the generator: `hero.bar.hoursParsed.open` and `contact.hours.parsed.open` were "09:00"; now "9:00" (`close` stays "21:00"). The generator now strips the zero.
- New data uses plain numbers: article ids `art-1` ... `art-21`, segment `order` 1 ... 4, tables in the manifests start at 1.
- Not numbering, so untouched: the price-list date `15.03.2026.` (shown verbatim by the old site), `pricing.validFromISO` (an ISO date) and the generation timestamps.
